Inroads 2 product map

Every flow in today's app, merged with the agent ideas, as one product. What carries over unchanged, what changes shape, what's new, and how we build it alongside the current app without breaking it.

The current app has 35 screens, 10 AI features, 7 background jobs and 30 tables. All of that is accounted for below. Companion page: Inroads redesign directions.

The idea in one line

Inroads stops reminding you and starts doing it, with your say-so

Four surfaces, not thirty screens

Brief: what matters today, in Inroads' own words. Inbox: what Inroads wants to do for you, to approve. People, Events and Goals: the record, for when you want to look. One input everywhere: type or speak anything.

Every nudge becomes an action

"40 days since you spoke with Adaeze" becomes a ready congratulations note on WhatsApp. Today's nudge logic stays; it now proposes an action with the work already done.

You set how much it does alone

Per person and per kind of action: suggest, draft for approval, or just do it. Everything it does is logged and can be undone.

You can still do it all yourself

Every manual action in today's app stays: add a person, log a touch, add a promise or event, edit anything, research or look up on demand, sort, merge, draft a message. The agent adds a faster path; it never removes one. Turn it off and Inroads 2 works like a better-looking Inroads 1.

Built on what works

The data, sync, research, prompts, scoring, nudges and jobs all carry over. The new parts are the agent loop, the inbox, the trust controls and a new interface.

Navigation

How the app is organised

Five tabs today (Today, People, Discover, Events, Goals) plus a dozen side pages. In v2 the tabs become Brief, Inbox, People, Events and Goals. Discover and Ask are reached from the one input, and also keep their own pages. Suggestions, Duplicates and Sort show up as Inbox items when there's something to do, and keep their full review pages for when you want to go through everything yourself.

The engine

The agent loop

Everything the app already notices (a sync, a job change, a promise falling due, an event ending) becomes a signal. The agent turns signals into proposed actions with the work done, and your trust settings decide which go to the Inbox and which just happen.

Journeys

Four moments, end to end

Monday, 8am

The weekly brief

  1. Push: "Your week: 4 meetings, 2 promises due, 6 actions ready."
  2. The Brief opens with a short message and this week's goal progress.
  3. Inbox: approve 4 drafts in a minute (congrats to Adaeze, follow-up to Tunde, a nudge to Kemi who owes you, an intro ask to Dayo).
  4. Each sends through WhatsApp or email and is logged as a touch.

Thursday, after a mixer

Follow-up the same night

  1. Inroads knows the event ended and asks: "How was Lagos Fintech Mixer? Who did you meet?"
  2. You answer with a 40-second voice note.
  3. It adds 3 people, logs the touches, records "intro Ada to Tola by Friday", and starts research.
  4. By morning: three follow-ups and the intro opt-in to Tola wait in the Inbox.

Over 10 weeks

Raising a seed round

  1. You say "raise $1.5M by March". Inroads maps funds that fit, who you know at each, and warm paths.
  2. Every Monday it proposes this week's moves: three intro asks, two events, one follow-up.
  3. Replies move people through the pipeline automatically.
  4. The weekly brief reports progress and what to change.

Any day

Being the connector

  1. Inroads notices Chidera is hiring a head of people and Ife is looking.
  2. Inbox: "You could introduce them. Ask both?"
  3. One tap sends each a short opt-in. When both say yes, the intro goes out.
  4. Both relationships warm up, and it shows in your weekly progress.

Building it

A separate version, same foundations

Inroads 2 lives in the same codebase and database, at its own address (for example /v2, or later next.inroads…). You and Azeez switch between the two from Settings. The current app keeps working and keeps getting fixes until v2 replaces it.

Reused as is

  • Database, sign-in, Spaces and security rules
  • Sync, imports, dedupe, merge
  • All AI features, prompts, cost limits, batch refresh
  • Nudge, strength, recurrence, goal and intro logic (src/domain)
  • Background jobs and push

New underneath

  • Proposed actions (what, for whom, why, the draft, status)
  • Trust settings (per action, per person)
  • Activity log with undo
  • The planner job that turns signals into actions
  • Sending and booking, behind your approval

New on top

  • A design system for the chosen direction
  • The v2 shell: Brief, Inbox, People, Events, Goals
  • The one input (text and voice)
  • Trust and activity screens

Rules for the build

  • Database changes only add things, so the current app is never broken
  • Every v2 screen is tested like today's (unit, database, browser)
  • A checklist of every manual action in v1; v2 isn't done until each one works
  • Nothing sends without approval until trust settings ship
  • Same PR-per-change, merge-when-green rhythm
Phase 0

Direction and design system

You pick the direction. I build its design system page (colours, type, components) and three key screens as clickable mocks. You and Azeez react before any build.

Phase 1

Shell, Brief and Inbox, read-only

v2 shell and navigation. The Brief and Inbox built from today's nudges, with drafts ready. "Send" opens WhatsApp or email with the text, exactly as today, and logs the touch. No new permissions.

Phase 2

Records and the one input

People, person record, Events, Goals and Settings redrawn in v2, with every manual action from v1 (checked off against the list). The one input sits alongside capture, Ask and Discover. At this point v2 does everything v1 does, by hand.

Phase 3

The agent loop and trust

The planner job, trust settings and activity log. After-event follow-ups, promise follow-through, intro asks, reason-led check-ins. Optional calendar booking and email sending, each asked for when first needed.

Phase 4

Campaigns and matchmaking

Goals run as weekly campaigns. Intros you could make, proposed weekly. Voice rehearsal before key meetings.

Later

WhatsApp number and agent-to-agent

An Inroads WhatsApp number (needs Meta approval), then agent-to-agent intros once there are enough users. We design the permissions for this in Phase 3 so we don't have to redo them.

Costs and risks

What to watch

AI cost

An agent planning all day costs more than reminders. The planner runs on events (a sync, an event ending), uses the cheaper model for routine drafts and the half-price batch service overnight. We set a monthly budget per user before Phase 3.

Sending as you

A wrong message to an investor costs more than a missed reminder. Default is "draft and ask"; "just do it" only for actions you choose; never LinkedIn automation; every send logged.

Other people's data

Researching people and recording voice notes about them falls under Nigeria's data protection law (NDPA 2023) and GDPR for others. We keep research to public professional info, as today, and add clear notes in Settings.

Two apps at once

Running v1 and v2 side by side doubles some UI work for a while. Fixes go into the shared server code so both get them; v1 gets UI fixes only until v2 covers everything (end of Phase 2).
